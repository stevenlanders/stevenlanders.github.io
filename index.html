Steven Landers

Principal Engineer — distributed systems, financial-market infrastructure, security

I'm a principal-level engineer with 20 years of experience building distributed, security-sensitive systems — consensus protocols, parallel execution engines, fraud detection, and regulated financial infrastructure. At Sei Labs I lead consensus and exchange-platform work; before that I helped grow Forta from a laptop prototype into a 12,000-node decentralized security network.

Highlights

Sei Giga — designed for ~5 gigagas/sec, roughly 200,000 TPS

I led the design, prototyping, and rollout of a multi-proposer blockchain architecture based on a modified Autobahn consensus protocol, and lead the five-engineer consensus and execution team delivering it incrementally to mainnet.

First parallelized EVM in the Sei ecosystem

I designed and implemented Block-STM parallel transaction execution on Sei's Cosmos-based EVM — deterministic state-conflict detection with a sequential fallback to preserve correctness under pathological conflict and denial-of-service scenarios.

Forta — laptop prototype to ~12,000 nodes, $23M a16z raise

As principal architect and technical leader, I helped grow a decentralized transaction-monitoring and threat-detection network to 900+ detection bots across Ethereum, BNB Smart Chain, Avalanche, Polygon, Arbitrum, and Optimism, serving 13,000+ subscribers.

Regulated exchange infrastructure

I lead hands-on development of a centralized exchange platform being prepared to support Designated Contract Market and Futures Commission Merchant license applications — trading systems, market operations, security controls, and production readiness.
